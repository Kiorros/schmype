### Purpose
Fun way to practive typing - gamify self-improvement.

### Software
Godot, Blender

### Platform
PC

### Genre
Typing, Vertical Scrolling Shooter

## Concept

### Core Loop
- 26 Stages
- Each stage composed of:
  - Waves of regular enemies
  - Boss
  - Choice of powerup
- Winning the game unlocks harder difficulty (harder/longer words)

### Themes
Stages are alphabet themed (A-Z).

### Primary Mechanics
Typing words to dodge enemies and fire weapons

### Secondary Mechanics
Selecting powerups

## Art
 - Top-down view of 3D assets
 - Low-poly, abstract spaceship models
 - Black and white with some splashes of color for highlights and effects

## Audio
 - Upbeat chip-tunes style music
 - Simple, cartoon style sound effects
